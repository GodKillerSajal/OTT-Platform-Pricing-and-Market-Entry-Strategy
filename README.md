# Analysis, Pricing and Market Entry Strategy for an OTT Platform

**Context & Affiliation**: Society of Business / Business Baazigar Round 2, IIT Roorkee  
**Domain**: Strategy Consulting, Subscription Economics, Freemium Funnel Optimization & Content LTV  
**Focus Subject**: Company X (Freemium streaming platform with a 30M user base, 3M active subscribers, and a single rigid ₹150/mo plan).

---

## 1. Problem Statement & Strategic Diagnosis
Company X had achieved pan-India reach with 30 million active users, but faced subscriber stagnation in metro markets due to aggressive competition from tiered incumbents (Hotstar, Netflix, Zee5) and lack of price differentiation. With Indian online video content investments projected to surge from $1.3B to $4.2B, Company X required a data-backed market entry and tiered pricing overhaul to unlock tier-2/3 regional growth and monetize freemium viewers.

---

## 2. Strategic & Analytical Execution
- **4-Tier Subscription Architecture**: Restructured the single ₹150/mo plan into a tiered matrix tailored to price sensitivity:
  1. *Ad-supported Mobile* @ ₹79/mo (capturing high-volume, price-elastic student/tier-2 users)
  2. *Ad-Free Basic Mobile + TV* @ ₹99/mo (single screen, 1080p)
  3. *Ad-Free Standard* @ ₹149/mo (dual device access)
  4. *Ad-Free Premium* @ ₹199/mo (4-screen 4K multi-device family plan)
- **Freemium-to-Paid Conversion Funnel**: Modelled conversion elasticity across the 30M user base, evaluating promotional 24-hr trial windows, festive deals, and regional Indic content acquisitions.
- **LTV & Churn Trade-off Modeling**: Simulated ARPU vs. customer churn dynamics across subscription tiers, projecting an optimized price-point balance between subscriber retention and content licensing ROI.

---

## 3. Key Quantitative Metrics & Outcomes
- **4-Tier Pricing Implemented**: Slashed entry price barrier by 47% (₹150 down to ₹79), expanding addressable audience.
- **22.5% Projected Paid User Conversion Lift**: Driven by regional content bundles and mobile-only introductory pricing.
- **9.4% Projected Reduction in Annual Subscriber Churn**: Mitigated through personalized tier downgrades instead of outright cancellations.
